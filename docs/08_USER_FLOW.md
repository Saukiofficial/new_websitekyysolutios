# 08 — User Flow

## Guest

```text
Landing Page
  ↓
Marketplace
  ↓
Search / Category / Filter
  ↓
Product Detail
  ↓
View Product
  ↓
Buy Now
  ↓
Login / Register
```

Guest does NOT need login for:
- browsing,
- search,
- category,
- product detail,
- seller store,
- services,
- portfolio,
- blog.

## Buyer

```text
Login
 ↓
Product Detail
 ↓
Buy Now
 ↓
Checkout
 ↓
Payment
 ↓
Payment Success
 ↓
Order Created / Paid
 ↓
Product Access
 ↓
My Products
 ↓
Access Product
```

## Seller

```text
Register/Login
 ↓
Seller onboarding
 ↓
Create Store
 ↓
Create Product
 ↓
Add external delivery link
 ↓
Submit
 ↓
Admin moderation
 ↓
Approved
 ↓
Published
 ↓
Sales
 ↓
Earnings
 ↓
Withdrawal
```

## Admin

```text
Login
 ↓
Admin Dashboard
 ↓
Moderate Sellers / Products
 ↓
Monitor Orders / Payments
 ↓
Manage Commission
 ↓
Process Withdrawals
 ↓
Manage Reports / CMS / Settings
```
