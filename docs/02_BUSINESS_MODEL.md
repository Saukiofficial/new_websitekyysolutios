# 02 — Business Model

## 1. Positioning

**KyySolutions — Digital Technology Services & Products**

Konsep komunikasi:
**Build with us. Buy ready-made solutions.**

## 2. Revenue Streams

### Development Services
Pendapatan dari project custom:
- website,
- application,
- software,
- design,
- integration,
- maintenance.

### Marketplace Commission
Platform mengambil persentase dari transaksi seller.

Contoh:
- Product: Rp500.000
- Commission: 10%
- Platform: Rp50.000
- Seller: Rp450.000

### Future Revenue
Dapat dikembangkan menjadi:
- featured product,
- promoted listing,
- seller subscription,
- premium seller account,
- license upgrade,
- customization,
- maintenance,
- deployment service.

## 3. Marketplace Strategy

### Phase 1 — First Party
KyySolutions menjual produk milik sendiri.

### Phase 2 — Curated Marketplace
Developer eksternal dapat menjadi seller melalui approval.

### Phase 3 — Open Marketplace
Developer dapat mendaftar dan mengirim produk untuk moderation.

## 4. Storefront

Setiap seller dapat memiliki public storefront, misalnya:

`kyysolutions.com/@username`

Store menampilkan:
- profile,
- description,
- social links,
- products,
- ratings,
- total sales jika diizinkan,
- badges/status seller.

## 5. Buyer Acquisition

Sumber traffic:
- SEO product pages,
- social media,
- developer community,
- content/blog,
- portfolio,
- direct service clients,
- referrals.

## 6. Services ↔ Marketplace Synergy

Marketplace → Services:
Buyer membeli source code lalu membutuhkan customization.

Services → Marketplace:
Client membutuhkan solusi cepat dan dapat diarahkan ke existing product sebagai basis development.

## 7. Seller Payout Concept

Transaction:

`Buyer Payment → Payment Verification → Platform Commission → Seller Balance → Withdrawal`

Disarankan ada:
- pending balance,
- available balance,
- withdrawal history.

## 8. Trust & Moderation

KyySolutions harus memiliki:
- product moderation,
- seller verification,
- report product,
- report seller,
- refund/dispute policy,
- license information,
- changelog/version information.

## 9. Strategic Principle

Jangan membangun marketplace sebagai fitur terpisah dari brand KyySolutions. Pada fase awal, marketplace dan services harus saling memperkuat.
